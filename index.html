Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSEA) Standards

We are committed to the highest standards of child protection and the prevention of child sexual exploitation and abuse (CSEA) across all our operations, services, and platforms. Our Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (EASI) standards are designed to provide clear guidance on safeguarding children and ensuring that any incidents of exploitation and abuse are prevented, reported, and addressed in a timely and transparent manner.

Key Components of Our CSEA Standards

1. Prevention:

  • Implementing robust screening and vetting procedures for all staff and volunteers who interact with children.
  • Providing comprehensive training on child safeguarding, identifying risk factors, and reporting procedures.
  • Promoting a safe and inclusive environment that discourages harmful behaviors.

2. Protection:

  • Establishing clear reporting mechanisms for suspected or actual cases of child sexual exploitation and abuse.
  • Ensuring access to confidential, survivor-centered support services for victims.
  • Adopting zero-tolerance policies for any form of abuse, exploitation, or misconduct.

3. Accountability:

  • Holding individuals and organizations accountable for upholding safeguarding principles and protocols.
  • Ensuring transparency in investigations and follow-up actions regarding any incidents or allegations.
  • Reporting violations to relevant authorities and cooperating fully with law enforcement agencies.

4. Collaboration:

  • Working closely with national and international child protection agencies, law enforcement, and non-governmental organizations to prevent child sexual exploitation and abuse.
  • Sharing best practices and knowledge to continuously improve our standards and policies.

5. Continuous Improvement:

  • Regularly reviewing and updating our CSEA policies to adapt to emerging risks and challenges.
  • Conducting audits and evaluations to ensure compliance with CSEA standards and identifying areas for improvement.

Reporting and Support

Our commitment to these standards reflects our determination to protect children from sexual exploitation and abuse, ensuring that all children grow up in a safe, supportive, and nurturing environment.
